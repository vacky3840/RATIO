# RATIO

> A watch from a world where social media replaced the state. Your rank is
> followers divided by following, and everyone can read it off your wrist.

## The rule

Standing is a ratio: **followers ÷ following**. Being watched earns.
Watching costs. Follow someone and your own number falls.

That single mechanic produces the whole world. At the top are accounts who
follow nobody — watched by millions, paying nothing back. At the bottom,
colonies follow each other in closed loops, spending everything they have on
people who cannot afford to look back.

Nobody is silenced here. Attention is simply priced, and most people cannot
afford to give it away.

## What's in this repo

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `attention_watch_prototype.html` | The wearable. Ratio, colony, ranking ladder, and a live cross-colony feed. Post, refuse a directive, watch your standing fall. |
| `ratio_world.html` | The world at a distance. Two colonies across one divide — arguments, doomscrolling, enforcement, and the people nobody renders. |

Both are single self-contained files. Open either in a browser; nothing to install.

## Controls

**Watch** — drag to turn, scroll to zoom. Tap the feed tabs to switch between
your colony and all colonies. Tap a post to open that account. Space to post.

**World** — drag to look, scroll to move in, click any person to read them.
